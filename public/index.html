qr-bot

Link WhatsApp by QR code. The server attempts private WhatsApp delivery and also gives you one browser view with separate copyable session parts.

QR session method:
1. Click Generate QR Code.
2. In WhatsApp, open Settings → Linked Devices.
3. Tap Link a Device and scan the QR code.
4. Copy each displayed session variable separately into Railway’s Raw Editor.
