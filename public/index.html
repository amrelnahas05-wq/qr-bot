qr-bot

Link WhatsApp by QR code. Your Railway session variables are delivered privately in WhatsApp, never on this page.

QR session method:
1. Click Generate QR Code.
2. In WhatsApp, open Settings → Linked Devices.
3. Tap Link a Device and scan the QR code.
4. Open your WhatsApp chat to receive the private Railway session messages.
